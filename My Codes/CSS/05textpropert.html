Chennai Super Kings

Injuries/Unavailability:

With Gaikwad out of the tournament, it will be interesting to see who CSK pick to replace him in the XI. While they do have options in the squad, Rahul Tripathi might be the current favourite to replace his statemate. Young wicketkeeper Vansh Bedi is another option they might look at to shore up the middle order in place of the third pacer.

While CSK have not used Matheesha Pathirana with the new ball, there is a case for that to happen against Sunil Narine. The West Indian allrounder loves facing spin and the hosts will look to remove him early before he faces their spin trio. R Ashwin has had his struggles this season but enjoys a very good matchup against the in-form KKR skipper Ajinkya Rahane.

Probable XII:

Rachin Ravindra, Devon Conway, Rahul Tripathi, Shivam Dube, Vijay Shankar, Ravindra Jadeja, MS Dhoni (c&wk), R Ashwin, Noor Ahmad, Matheesha Pathirana, Khaleel Ahmed, Mukesh Choudhary/Anshul Kamboj

Kolkata Knight Riders

Apart from that unbeaten 97 against Rajasthan Royals, Quinton de Kock has looked poor in the tournament so far. Rahmanullah Gurbaz did have a long session in the nets on the eve of the game and with familiarity against CSK's biggest threat Noor Ahmad, the Afghanistan wicketkeeper might get his chance. Don't rule out the possibility of Moeen Ali being their third spinner ahead of Spencer Johnson.

Tactics & Match-ups:

Even at the age of 43, Dhoni looks like CSK's most reliable power-hitter down the order. KKR will be tempted to hold back an over each from their spin twins to bowl to Dhoni at the death. The veteran has a poor strike-rate of 52.7 against Narine and has been dismissed thrice by Chakaravarthy in four innings. Jadeja also has a poor record against Narine with a strike rate less than 100, thus strengthening KKR's case to save a couple of overs for CSK's finishers.

Probable XII:

Rahmanullah Gurbaz (wk), Sunil Narine, Ajinkya Rahane (c), Angkrish Raghuvanshi, Venkatesh Iyer, Rinku Singh, Andre Russell, Ramandeep Singh, Moeen Ali/Spencer Johnson, Harshit Rana, Varun Chakaravarthy, Vaibhav Arora

Did you know:

- This is only the third time CSK have lost four or more games in a row in an IPL season. The other two occasions were in 2010 (where they won the title) and 2022 (where they finished 9th) - Ravindra Jadeja's average with the ball in CSK's wins is 21.98 but it jumps to 49.34 in defeats - KKR's catching efficiency is 87.5 this season, which is the second highest. By contrast, CSK's is the second lowest at 67.6
